How to add on iPhone / iPad

iOS Mail strips most formatting on paste. Follow these steps to keep your signature looking right:

  1. Click Copy for iOS below, then send the signature to yourself by email from your computer.
  2. Open that email on your iPhone, tap and hold the signature, choose Select All → Copy.
  3. Open Settings → Mail → Signature, delete the existing text and paste the copied signature.
  4. Shake your phone until the "Undo Change Attributes" popup appears, then tap Undo.
  5. Tap the back arrow to save. Send a test email to confirm formatting.
iPad without shake? Enable it in Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Shake to Undo. Logo image must stay reachable on the public CDN — don't edit the signature after pasting.
